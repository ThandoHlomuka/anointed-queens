Delivery & Returns

Shipping & returns, made easy

From dispatch to doorstep to wardrobes - this is exactly how your bag reaches you.

Delivery

Orders are shipped securely via The Courier Guy, fully trackable.

  • Free courier on orders over R1,500
  • Small fee on orders under R1,500
  • Usually 2-5 business days across South Africa
  • Collect in person from our Sandton atelier by appointment
Custom orders

Shipping times

Ready-to-ship bags leave within 2 business days. Custom bags are made to order and typically ship in 2-4 weeks from design confirmation.

You'll get tracking details the moment your bag is handed to the courier.

Returns & exchanges

Ready-to-ship bags can be returned or exchanged unused, with original tags, within 7 days of delivery. To start a return, simply message us with your order number.

What's not returnable

Because each one is made to your design, custom bag orders are non-refundable once production begins. Deposits are refundable before production starts.
