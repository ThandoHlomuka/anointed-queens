Our Story

Purpose-built, hand-carried

Anointed Queens exists for one reason: to give everyday queens bags that fit their real lives - and their real days. Not mass-produced handbags that look just like everyone else's, but pieces designed around what you carry, where you go and who you are.

It starts with a conversation about your day - work and laptop, gym and errands, evenings out. From there we build: the right size, the right pockets, the right materials. Premium full-grain leather, gold-toned hardware, stitching done slowly and carefully by hand in our South African atelier.

We believe a bag shouldn't fight your lifestyle - it should support it. So whether it's a quick ready-to-ship favourite or a fully custom commission, every Anointed Queens piece is made to be carried, kept and passed on.

What "Anointed" means

Set apart for purpose

To anoint is to set something apart - to dedicate it to what it was made for. That is exactly what we hope every Anointed Queens bag is: set apart for the life it was designed around, carried by a queen who chose it on purpose.
