Our Story

Crowned in craft, made to be kept

Anointed Queens began with a simple belief: a bag should never be an afterthought. It should be the companion that carries your story - your keys, your letters, your promises - and it should feel like it was made for one person on earth.

Every Anointed Queens piece begins as a conversation. You choose the silhouette, the hide, the colour of the gold, the way it closes and who it remembers on its lining. Our atelier then cuts, stitches, edge-paints and sets hardware by hand - no factory line, no shortcuts, no two pieces identical.

We favour full-grain leather with real character, hardware that gleams in antique or polished gold, and finishing that improves for a lifetime. And because craft is built on trust, every commission - from first deposit to final approval - is protected by two-step escrow. You pay only what you approve.

Hand-cut

Every panel is laid out and cut by hand, minimising waste and honouring the hide's natural character.

Small-batch

We make a limited number of pieces each month. Craft cannot be rushed.

Escrow first

Your money is held until you confirm. The atelier is paid only after you approve the finish.

Made in SA

Crafted proudly in South Africa using responsibly sourced hides.
